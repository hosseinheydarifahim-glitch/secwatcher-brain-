# Audience

> SecWatcher builds products for organizations, but ultimately protects the people who trust those organizations.

---

# Purpose

An audience is not everyone who benefits from a product.

It is everyone involved in the decision, purchase, deployment, use, and outcome of that product.

SecWatcher serves multiple audiences.

Each has different goals, different problems, and requires different communication.

Understanding these differences is essential for building products, writing content, and positioning the company.

---

# Primary Audience

## Fintech Security Leaders

These are the people who directly experience the problems SecWatcher solves.

Typical roles include:

- Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
- Head of Security
- Security Manager
- Security Engineer
- Threat Intelligence Analyst
- Security Operations Lead

### Goals

- Detect threats earlier.
- Reduce financial fraud.
- Protect customers.
- Improve visibility.
- Reduce incident response time.

### Challenges

- Limited visibility outside organizational infrastructure.
- Increasing phishing campaigns.
- Brand impersonation.
- Resource constraints.
- Pressure to reduce operational risk.

### What They Value

- Technical accuracy.
- Reliable intelligence.
- Transparent limitations.
- Actionable information.
- Evidence over marketing.

# Communication Strategy

Different audiences require different communication.

| Audience | Primary Need | Communication Style |
|----------|--------------|---------------------|
| Security Engineers | Technical accuracy | Deep technical content |
| Security Managers | Operational visibility | Intelligence and workflows |
| CTOs | Architecture and scalability | Product capabilities |
| Executives | Business impact | Risk and trust |
| Researchers | Evidence and methodology | Transparent research |
| General Public | Awareness | Clear, accessible explanations |

The facts remain consistent.

Only the presentation changes.

---

# Audience Principles

## Speak to Expertise

Never simplify technical truth.

Adapt language without reducing accuracy.

---

## Respect Intelligence

Assume the audience is capable of critical thinking.

Avoid fear-based messaging.

Avoid exaggerated claims.

---

## Build Trust Before Selling

Useful information creates credibility.

Credibility creates trust.

Trust creates customers.

---

## Teach From Experience

Publish only what SecWatcher has earned the right to discuss.

Authority comes from original observation, not repetition.

---

## Separate Buyers From Beneficiaries

Organizations purchase products.

People experience the outcome.

Products should create value for both.

---

# Audience Evolution

As products expand, audiences will expand.

The company's identity should remain constant.

Every new audience must still fit the mission:

**Strengthening trust across the Iranian fintech ecosystem through intelligent security products.**
