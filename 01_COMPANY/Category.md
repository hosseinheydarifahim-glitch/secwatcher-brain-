# Category

> SecWatcher operates in the category of **Fintech Security Intelligence**.

---

# Purpose

A category defines how the market understands a company.

It answers a simple question:

> **"What kind of company is this?"**

A category is not a description of products.

It is the mental model customers use to place the company.

Choosing the wrong category creates confusion.

Choosing the right category creates clarity.

---

# Our Category

**Fintech Security Intelligence**

SecWatcher develops intelligent security products that help financial technology companies detect, understand, and respond to digital threats.

The company combines:

- Security
- Threat Intelligence
- Fraud Detection
- AI
- Local Research

into a single platform focused on the Iranian fintech ecosystem.

---

# Why Not "Cybersecurity Company"

"Cybersecurity" is too broad.

A cybersecurity company could build:

- Firewalls
- Antivirus
- SIEM
- Endpoint Detection
- Vulnerability Scanners
- Identity Management
- Cloud Security
- Network Security

This category says nothing about what makes the company unique.

SecWatcher intentionally avoids this positioning.

---

# Why Not "Fraud Detection Company"

Fraud is an outcome.

It is not always the observable problem.

Many fraud incidents begin as:

- Phishing campaigns
- Brand impersonation
- Fake payment pages
- Credential theft
- Social engineering
- Infrastructure abuse

SecWatcher focuses on detecting the intelligence surrounding these activities before financial damage occurs.

Fraud detection is one capability.

It is not the entire company.

---

# Why Not "Threat Intelligence Company"

Traditional threat intelligence companies primarily focus on:

- Malware
- Nation-state activity
- Vulnerabilities
- Indicators of Compromise (IOCs)
- Enterprise security

SecWatcher operates in a narrower and more specialized domain.

Its intelligence is designed specifically for financial technology organizations.

---

# Why Fintech Security Intelligence

This category accurately reflects the company's long-term direction.

It allows future products to remain consistent with the company's identity.

Examples include:

- Phishing Intelligence
- Brand Protection
- Fraud Intelligence
- Risk Intelligence
- Device Intelligence
- Transaction Intelligence
- Identity Intelligence

Each product belongs naturally within the same category.

---

# Category Boundaries

SecWatcher focuses on security problems related to digital financial services.

Included:

- Phishing
- Brand Impersonation
- Fraud Detection
- Threat Intelligence
- Risk Analysis
- Security Intelligence
- Fraud Research
- Detection Platforms

Excluded:

- Network Administration
- IT Operations
- Infrastructure Monitoring
- Endpoint Protection
- Managed IT Services
- General Consulting
- Consumer Antivirus

These domains may interact with our products but do not define our category.

---

# Long-Term Position

The category should remain stable even as products evolve.

Products may change.

Technologies may change.

Customer needs may change.

The category should continue answering one question:

> "What business is SecWatcher fundamentally in?"

The answer remains:

**Fintech Security Intelligence.**

---

# Internal Definition

SecWatcher is not defined by a single product.

SecWatcher is defined by a category of expertise.

Products are expressions of that expertise.

The category outlives every individual product.

---

# Category Principles

1. Stay focused on fintech security.

2. Build intelligence before features.

3. Prioritize localized understanding over generalized coverage.

4. Let products expand within the category, not outside it.

5. Protect category clarity as the company grows.

---

**SecWatcher is a Fintech Security Intelligence company that builds intelligent, research-driven security products for the Iranian financial ecosystem.**
