# Mission

> Build intelligent security products that reduce digital fraud across the Iranian fintech ecosystem.

---

# Mission Statement

SecWatcher develops security products and intelligence platforms that help Iranian fintech companies detect, understand, and respond to digital fraud before it causes financial loss.

We focus on solving localized security problems that cannot be effectively addressed by global solutions.

Every product we build should make the Iranian digital financial ecosystem more trustworthy, resilient, and secure.

---

# Why We Exist

Digital fraud evolves continuously.

Attackers adapt faster than traditional security solutions.

Iranian fintech companies face unique threats shaped by local language, infrastructure, payment systems, regulations, and user behavior.

Generic global security products rarely understand these local characteristics.

SecWatcher exists to close this gap.

---

# What We Build

SecWatcher builds software products that transform security knowledge into practical tools.

These products may include:

- Threat Intelligence Platforms
- Fraud Detection Systems
- Brand Protection Tools
- Security Intelligence Services
- Future products addressing emerging fintech security challenges

Products are expressions of the mission, not the mission itself.

---

# What We Solve

We help organizations answer questions such as:

- Are customers being targeted outside our infrastructure?
- Is our brand being impersonated?
- Are new fraud campaigns emerging?
- What risks are increasing across the ecosystem?
- How can threats be detected earlier?

---

# Who We Serve

Primary customers are organizations operating within the Iranian fintech ecosystem, including:

- Payment Service Providers
- Payment Gateways
- Digital Wallets
- Cryptocurrency Exchanges
- Financial Platforms
- Banks
- Insurance Technology Companies
- Other financial service providers

---

# How We Work

Every solution we build should satisfy four principles:

## Local First

Our products are designed around the realities of the Iranian digital ecosystem.

Localization is a core capability, not an afterthought.

---

## Intelligence Driven

Decisions should be based on evidence, telemetry, and research.

Not assumptions.

---

## Product Focused

We solve problems by building scalable products rather than one-off services whenever possible.

---

## Continuous Learning

Every product should generate new knowledge.

Every new insight should improve future products.

Knowledge compounds over time.

---

# What We Do Not Do

SecWatcher is not a general cybersecurity company.

We do not attempt to solve every security problem.

We focus on building products where our knowledge creates a meaningful competitive advantage.

The scope of the company expands only when new products strengthen the mission.

---

# Success

The mission succeeds when:

- Iranian fintech companies identify fraud earlier.
- Customers experience fewer successful attacks.
- Security teams make faster and better decisions.
- Products continuously improve from accumulated intelligence.
- Trust in the Iranian digital financial ecosystem increases.

---

# Mission Principles

1. Solve real problems before building technology.

2. Build products that scale knowledge.

3. Prefer long-term intelligence over short-term features.

4. Local expertise is a strategic advantage.

5. Every product should strengthen the ecosystem.

6. The mission remains constant even as products evolve.
