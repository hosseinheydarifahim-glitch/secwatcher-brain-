# Positioning

> SecWatcher is an intelligence-driven fintech security company that builds localized security products for the Iranian financial ecosystem.

---

# Positioning Statement

SecWatcher develops intelligent security products that help Iranian fintech companies detect, understand, and respond to digital fraud.

Unlike global cybersecurity vendors that build generalized solutions for international markets, SecWatcher focuses exclusively on the realities of the Iranian fintech ecosystem.

Our competitive advantage comes from local knowledge, continuous research, and products designed specifically for Iranian threats, infrastructure, language, and user behavior.

# The Problem We Solve

Most security tools observe what happens inside an organization's systems.

Many fraud campaigns begin somewhere else.

Examples include:

- Fake domains
- Fake payment pages
- Brand impersonation
- Credential theft
- External fraud infrastructure

Organizations often discover these attacks only after customers report them.

SecWatcher exists to reduce that gap.

---

# Why We Exist

Iranian fintech companies operate in an environment with unique characteristics:

- Persian language

- Regional infrastructure
- Country-specific attack patterns
- Local threat actors

Global security vendors optimize for global scale.

SecWatcher optimizes for local understanding.
