# PhishBan

> Detect phishing campaigns targeting Iranian organizations before they become customer incidents.

---

# Overview

PhishBan is the first product developed by SecWatcher.

It is an intelligence-driven phishing detection platform designed specifically for the Iranian fintech ecosystem.

Rather than monitoring an organization's internal infrastructure, PhishBan continuously observes the external internet to identify phishing campaigns, fake domains, impersonation attempts, and fraudulent infrastructure targeting Iranian brands.

Its objective is simple:

**Reduce the time between the appearance of a phishing campaign and the moment the affected organization becomes aware of it.**

# The Problem

Organizations spend significant resources protecting their internal infrastructure.

However, many successful attacks occur completely outside their environment.

Examples include:

- Fake domains
- Fake payment gateways
- Brand impersonation
- Credential harvesting websites
- Social engineering campaigns

These attacks frequently remain invisible until:

- Customers report them.
- Financial losses occur.
- Brand reputation is damaged.

The organization cannot investigate what it cannot see.

PhishBan exists to reduce this visibility gap.

---

# The Core Idea

Infrastructure security answers:

> **What is happening inside my systems?**

PhishBan answers:

> **What is happening outside my systems that targets my customers?**

This distinction defines the product.

# Product Philosophy

PhishBan does not claim to prevent phishing.

It claims to detect phishing earlier.

This distinction is fundamental.

Detection creates visibility.

Visibility enables response.

Response reduces harm.

The product should never promise what its architecture cannot guarantee.

---

# Detection Philosophy

Detection is built from multiple independent signals.

Examples include:

- Domain characteristics
- Registration information
- DNS records
- SSL certificates
- HTML structure
- Visual similarity
- Brand references
- Hosting infrastructure
- Behavioral indicators

No individual signal determines the outcome.

Confidence increases through correlation.
